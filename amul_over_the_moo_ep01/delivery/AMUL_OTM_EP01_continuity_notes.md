- Three cuts deliberately move MOO forward in time and place (time ellipses):
  - **S01 → S02 (11.1 m):** from the meadow to the puddle on the path.

## Block

- It moves only in S06 and S07, frames 508–565:
  - S06: the first 1.2 cm creep, from 2.1 s into the shot.
  - S07: it slides on to a total of 6.5 cm, inside the brief's 5–8 cm.
- It is still before frame 508 and after frame 565. Debris near the block moves with it.

## Doors

- The east double doors are hinged at y = ±1.8 m and open inward, toward the camera. The two leaves rotate as mirror images.
- They are closed until frame 655, six frames into S09. They reach 30° by the end of S09 and 51.9° at the last frame.
- The opening angle only increases and both leaves match on every frame.
- Note: `config.BARN["doors"]["max_open_deg"]` (40) is not used by the animation. The keyed angles are set in `scripts/anim.py`.

## Brief prohibitions

- **Characters:** the only characters in the scene are MOO_01's three artwork views (side, rear, three-quarter).
- **Milk, delivery and products:** the scene has no milk, delivery props, product or end slate.
- **Shadow stand-in:** the 3D stand-in body that casts MOO's shadows is hidden from the camera.

## Known limits

- **Stand-in artwork:** MOO_01 was drawn from the written spec, because the approved character sheet and storyboard were not supplied. The swap procedure is in `README.md`.
- **Animatic frames:** these were rendered at 1280×720 and 10 samples with denoising. Low-light areas, such as the barn interior and the haze, show denoiser smoothing that the 128-sample 4K settings would not have.
