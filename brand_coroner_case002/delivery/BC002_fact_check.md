## Discrepancies flagged against the brief

- Scene 14: the brief pairs the going-concern disclosure with a September 2023 cash balance of about $462 million. The filings separate them: the going-concern warning first appears in the 10-Q filed August 8, 2023 (cash $205 million at June 30, 2023); the $462 million figure is cash at September 30, 2023, disclosed unaudited in an 8-K filed November 21, 2023, after the November 6 Chapter 11 petition. The film shows all four dates on a day-scaled timeline and labels the $462 million accordingly.
- Scene 09: the brief's '$1.61 billion net loss' is the net loss attributable to WeWork Companies Inc.; total 2018 net loss was $1.93 billion. Both are shown, with exact labels.
- Scene 01 and 12: the $47 billion private valuation has no primary filing in this pack. It is tagged REPORTED on screen until a primary citation (e.g. the SoftBank or WeWork January 2019 announcement) is attached.
- Typography: the brief lists on-screen statements in capitals but locks the identity as bold lowercase; all statements are set lowercase.
- Narration: the WeWork master documentary script was not supplied with the brief. The animatic is silent (temp sound design only) with narration markers per beat; no lines were written or voiced. Picture timing will need a conform pass once narration is recorded.
