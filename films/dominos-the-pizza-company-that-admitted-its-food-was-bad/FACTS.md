# Facts and sources: Brand Coroner · Case 012 · Domino's

Editorial rule: every historical claim is supported by documentary evidence. Domino's own releases and SEC filings are preferred. Same-store sales figures are for domestic (U.S.) stores, versus the prior-year period, as reported in each quarterly release.

| Scene | Claim (narration) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 01, 05, 07 | December 2009: the recipe change and a campaign built on customer criticism | Domino's Pizza press release, Dec 16, 2009, "Celebrating 50th Year, Domino's Pizza Gives Itself a Makeover": new crust, sauce and cheese; advertising by Crispin Porter + Bogusky "will include documentary-style anecdotes" about changing "the negative perceptions of some consumers"; "will be in all U.S. stores beginning December 27" |
| 01, 04 | Customers complained about the crust and the sauce | Ad quotes reported by *Slate* (Seth Stevenson, "Ad Report Card", Jan 11, 2010): "Domino's pizza crust to me is like cardboard"; "The sauce tastes like ketchup." Employees at headquarters read the feedback aloud |

## Storyboard items not used

- **Logos, packaging and the campaign's title card.** No Domino's logo, box art or "Pizza Turnaround" lettering is reproduced as a graphic. Names are set in the film's own type. Signage appears only where it is part of a dated photograph of a real store or vehicle.
- **Campaign footage and website captures.** The 2009 advertising is copyrighted, so it is not reproduced. The customer comments it used are quoted as reported by *Slate*, on a card labelled as a transcription.
- **The storyboard's customer quotes** ("The crust is bland", "The sauce is awful", "It just tastes cheap") and its checklist ("Crust: too bland … Overall: disappointing"). These don't match the documented ad wording, so they are not used.
- **Executives, a woman reacting, a man outside a store, a cook in a Domino's apron.** No verified, freely licensed photos of these exist, and the film does not stage them. It uses dated photographs of a Michigan store, delivery cars, the Ann Arbor headquarters, a Georgia store and two Domino's pizzas.
- **The 1960 storefront with a VW Beetle.** No verified 1960 photo is available. Scene 2 uses a 2007 photograph of the Domino's in Ypsilanti, Michigan.
- **A 2009–2010 online ordering screen.** None is freely licensed. Scene 12 shows the 10-K's own wording.
