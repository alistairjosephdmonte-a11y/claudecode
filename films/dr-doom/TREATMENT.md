# DR. DOOM — THE MAKING OF A MONSTER · treatment

60 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps. Kinetic typography only. Palette locked to **#FEC700 / #02462E / #000000**; depth comes from halftone dots, hatching, distress speckle, misregistered green offsets and opacity, never from new hues. No film grain in the master (grain would add off-palette pixels).

## Three candidate structures

1. **Seven chapters, one word per chapter** — each scene owned by a single hero word that becomes an object. Clear, but risks a slideshow.
2. **One continuous typographic machine** — every scene's debris becomes the next scene's raw material (VICTOR's fragments → LATVERIA's stones → GENIUS's shrapnel → the machine → WRONG's shards → the MASK's plates → DOOM's monument). **Chosen**: it makes "a human identity dismantled and rebuilt" literal, and gives every transition a visible cause.
3. **Countdown to the mask** — a timecode running down to the moment the mask locks. Strong tension, but it would steal attention from the words.

## Logline and arc

A name is built, broken by loss and pride, and rebuilt as a monument: VICTOR → DOOM.
Setup (VICTOR, Latveria, loss) → rise (genius, science + sorcery) → turn (the experiment, WRONG) → fall (PRIDE crushes FAILURE, the cracked FACE) → rebirth (MASK, DOOM).

## Benchmark

- Saul Bass and Kyle Cooper title sequences (*Anatomy of a Murder*, *Se7en*): **learn** type as action, cuts caused by graphic events, menace through restraint. **Don't take** their shapes, layouts or typefaces.
- Swiss / brutalist editorial posters: **learn** grid discipline, scale contrast, monospaced annotation as texture. 
- Not taken from Marvel: no comic panels, no costume drawing, no logos. The character is told through words only.

## Type system

- **Anton** (ultra-condensed heavy sans, OFL): hero words.
- **Barlow Condensed SemiBold** (narrow editorial caps, OFL): on-screen sentences.
- **JetBrains Mono** (OFL): technical annotations, diagrams, scene slugs.
- Safe area for phones: hero words inside x 10–88 %, y 12–80 %; nothing important in the bottom 18 % or the right edge.

## Native move

**Debris inheritance**: the pieces of each broken word are reused as the building material of the next shape. The signature shot is the experiment collapse: a single mis-slipped letter, 0.4 s of total silence, then WRONG.
