## Wording differences between the narration and the sources (narration kept verbatim)

- **"more than twelve percent" (S02):** the 10-K figure is 12.4% for fiscal 2026. Shown exactly.
- **"nearly 230 million units sold into distribution" (S03):** Take-Two says "sold-in" (shipped to retail and digital storefronts). The screen explains that this is not copies bought by players. The fiscal-2026 10-K figure is "over 225 million".
- **"demand exceeding historical benchmarks" (S11):** the 10-Q says "outpaced historical benchmarks". No pre-order units or revenue are disclosed, and the screen says so.
- **"September 2013":** the 10-K says GTA V was "released in 2013". The September launch month is common knowledge and is not in the Take-Two documents examined.
- **S12:** no budget figure is shown. The 10-K says only that hit titles "require increasing budgets for development and marketing".
