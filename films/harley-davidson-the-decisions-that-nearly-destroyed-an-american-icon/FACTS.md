## Wording differences between the narration and the sources (narration kept verbatim)

- **"merged with AMF" (S03):** histories describe it as AMF buying Harley-Davidson. On screen: "harley-davidson becomes part of amf".
- **"forty-five percentage points" (S05):** the memo says "45 percent ad valorem … above scheduled rates", i.e. 45 points added to the existing rate. On screen: "+45 points" and a note that this is not a 45% total rate.
- **"LiveWire sold 653" (S13):** the 10-K reports 653 and 612 as LiveWire unit shipments. The screen says "unit shipments".
- **"financial-services division helped keep the consolidated business profitable" (S12):** true for 2025 (net income $338.7M). The screen adds that HDFS's 2025 income was lifted by a one-off sale of receivables and that 2026 guidance is $45–60M.
- **S06 manufacturing improvements:** the specific figures come from the President's 1987 remarks and are labelled as such.
