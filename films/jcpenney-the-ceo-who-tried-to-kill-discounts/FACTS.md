## Storyboard items not used

- **Logos.** The storyboard shows the JCPenney and Apple logos and a "Fair and Square" sign. These are not reproduced as graphics; names are set in the film's own type. Store photographs that include building signage are shown as dated documentary photos.
- **"590+ promotional events".** Sources give 590 (and one AP report 529), not "more than 590"; the narration is kept verbatim, the screen shows "590" with the source and "counts vary".
- **"$4.28B sales drop".** Kept: $17,260M − $12,985M = $4,275M. Note that fiscal 2012 had 53 weeks.
- **Ron Johnson photo from 2011–13 and a Myron Ullman photo.** No freely licensed photos from his JCPenney tenure, or of Ullman, were found. Scenes 2 and 12 use a dated 2015 photo of Johnson; Ullman is named in text.
- **"Sephora at JCPenney" storefront.** The Commons photo used is dated 2022 and captioned as a *former* Sephora inside JCPenney.
