# Facts and sources: Brand Coroner · Case 011 · LEGO

Editorial rule: every historical claim is supported by documentary evidence. LEGO's own releases and annual reports are preferred. Figures are shown **as reported at the time**; LEGO's 2008 annual report later restated earlier years on a different basis, and the film shows that restatement on screen.

| Scene | Claim (narration) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 01, 12 | 2004 group loss of nearly DKK 2 billion | LEGO Group, 2005 results release (Feb 2006): "Results after tax, including discontinuing activities, improved considerably, from a loss of DKK 1,931 million in 2004 to a profit of DKK 505 million in 2005." |
| 02 | Billund; Ole Kirk Kristiansen founded the business in 1932; wooden toys to plastic bricks | LEGO history (lego.com); LEGO Annual Report 2008 (company background). On screen: stud-and-tube patent application filed Jan 28, 1958 (lego.com, "The stud and tube principle") |
| 03 | Late-1990s expansion including theme parks | RTÉ, July 13, 2005: four LEGOLAND parks (Billund, Windsor, Günzburg, San Diego). LEGOLAND Billund main entrance photographed c. 1968 (resort's own image archive) |
| 05 | 2003: group net loss ≈ DKK 935m; sales weakened sharply | LEGO Annual Report 2003: "Net sales fell by 26 percent from DKK 11.4 billion in 2002 to DKK 8.4 billion. The result was a pre-tax loss on earnings of DKK 1.4 billion." The DKK 935m net figure is the 2003 column in LEGO's later five-year summary (company profile financial highlights: 1,430 / 505 / (1,931) / (935) / 326 for 2006–2002); shown on screen with that attribution. Reuters (Aug 27, 2004): "the worst loss in its 72-year history" |
| 06 | 2004: Jørgen Vig Knudstorp became CEO | LEGO Annual Report 2008: Kjeld Kirk Kristiansen "CEO for the LEGO Group 1979-2004"; *The Telegraph* (Oct 2004) on Knudstorp as new chief executive |
| 08 | 2004 action plan cut costs ≈ DKK 1.52bn (20%) vs 2003 | LEGO Annual Report 2004 release (Apr 2005): "cutting its costs by DKK 1,523m (20%) below 2003 levels"; toy sales fell 7% from DKK 7,196m to DKK 6,704m |
| 08 | Workforce reduction of roughly 1,000 | *The Telegraph*, Oct 2004 (via LUGNET): "Lego has axed 1,000 of its 8,000-strong workforce". Earlier plans: up to 500 more jobs by 2006 (Berlingske Tidende, Mar 16, 2004, via Eurofound). Shown as "reported" |

## Storyboard items not used

- **Logos and product packaging.** No LEGO, LEGOLAND, Star Wars, City, Technic or Harry Potter logos or boxes are reproduced. Names are set in the film's own type. Bricks and fan-built models appear only in dated photographs.
- **Annual-report covers** with highlighted figures. Shown as typeset transcriptions labelled "not a facsimile", not as imitation documents.
- **"Billund 1933" storefront and factory floor with staff.** Not used; Scene 2 uses a dated 1957 photograph of Ole Kirk Christiansen and a 1957 family photo of a child building. Scene 8 uses a dated 2008 exterior of LEGO's Kladno factory, captioned with its place and date.
- **The "innovation with rules" checklist.** Shown as an illustrative framework labelled "editorial analysis, not a LEGO document".
- **LEGOLAND Billund entrance today.** Recent photos of the entrance sign are under a Commons deletion request (sign design copyright); the film uses the c. 1968 entrance photo released by the resort.
