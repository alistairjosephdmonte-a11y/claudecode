logo or the product.

| `wordmark.png` | supplied MANSCAPED™ wordmark (black on white JPG); white keyed to transparency, cropped | centre (720, 780), fits 600×120 | revealed at ≈ 4:55, after the sap-green wash, so the wash never tints it |

The supplied product image is a lifestyle photo with its own background rather than a cut-out, so it is shown whole
as a print instead of being cut out. To swap either file, replace it and re-render chapter 10:
`CHAPTERS="10" SKIP_VOICE=1 sh films/manscaped-trimming-through-the-ages/build.sh`.
