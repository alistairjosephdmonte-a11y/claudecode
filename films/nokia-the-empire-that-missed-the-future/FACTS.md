## Not used from the storyboard

- **Scene 13 chart "smartphone market share 38% / 16% / 3% (2007 / 2011 / 2013)":** this mixes Nokia's share of all mobile phones (38% in 2007) with smartphone-only shares and could not be verified as labelled. Scene 13 instead shows Nokia's share of all mobile devices from 2007 to 2012, with sources.
- **Scene 11 chart "Symbian sales 2010–2012":** Nokia does not report Symbian-only volumes for each year. Scene 11 shows Nokia's reported smartphone shipments for 2011 and 2012.
- **Company logos (Nokia, Microsoft, Windows Phone, App Store, Android, Symbian, MeeGo) and the handshake photo:** not reproduced. Names are set in the film's own type, and real dated photographs are used instead.
