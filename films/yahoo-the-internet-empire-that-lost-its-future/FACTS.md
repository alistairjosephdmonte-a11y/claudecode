# Facts and sources: Brand Coroner · Case 008 · Yahoo

Editorial rule: every historical claim is supported by documentary evidence. Primary documents (SEC filings, company releases) are preferred; where a story rests on press reporting, the film says so on screen ("reported", "accounts vary").

| Scene | Claim (narration) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Valued at more than $100 billion at the height of the dot-com boom | Yahoo's market capitalisation peaked at roughly $120–125 billion in January 2000 (all-time closing high $118.75 per share, Jan 3, 2000). Reported widely, e.g. New York Times / AP coverage of the 2016 Verizon deal ("valued at $125 billion at its peak in January 2000"); Jeremy Ring, *We Were Yahoo!* (2017). Shown on screen as "$100B+ · peak market value · Jan 2000" |
| 01, 02 | Core operating business sold to Verizon in 2017; closed June 13, 2017 | Yahoo release, June 13, 2017, "Yahoo Completes Sale of Operating Business; Company to Be Re-Named Altaba and Register as Investment Company"; Altaba Form 8-K, June 19, 2017 (sec.gov) |
| 02 | Amended purchase agreement: approximately $4.48 billion, subject to adjustments | Verizon & Yahoo joint release, Feb 21, 2017, "Verizon and Yahoo amend terms of definitive agreement": "now valued at approximately $4.48 billion in cash, subject to closing adjustments"; Yahoo Form 8-K (Feb 21, 2017): cash price $4,475,800,000 |
| 02 | Final accounting reflected a different closing consideration | Verizon Form 10-K FY2017, Exhibit 13: "aggregate purchase consideration … approximately $4.7 billion, including cash acquired of $0.2 billion" (cash paid to Yahoo at June 13, 2017: $4,723M; revised to $4,673M at Dec 31, 2017) |
| 02, 14 | Alibaba and Yahoo Japan stakes not part of the sale; holdings became Altaba | Yahoo release, June 13, 2017; Altaba 8-K: ≈15% of Alibaba Group, ≈36% of Yahoo Japan, cash and marketable debt securities. Name changed to Altaba Inc. on June 16, 2017 |
| 03 | 1994: Stanford graduate students Jerry Yang and David Filo; "Jerry and David's Guide to the World Wide Web" | Yahoo corporate history; Computing History (UK), "Yahoo founded January 1994"; Entrepreneur, "David Filo & Jerry Yang: The Chief Yahoos" |
| 03 | Went public in April 1996 | Yahoo Form 10-K FY1996 (initial public offering, April 1996; first trading day April 12, 1996, priced at $13) |
| 05 | Yahoo had search technology and search partnerships of its own | Yahoo used Inktomi (late 1990s) and then Google (from 2000) for algorithmic results; Yahoo 10-K FY2003/FY2004 |

| 09 | 2006 Facebook talks around a $1 billion valuation; no deal | Reported: *BusinessWeek* / *Wall Street Journal* (2006); David Kirkpatrick, *The Facebook Effect* (2010). ## Storyboard items not used

- **Logos and website screenshots.** The storyboard shows the Yahoo!, Google, Verizon, Microsoft, Facebook, Inktomi and Overture logos and early Yahoo/Google web pages. These are not reproduced; company names are set in the film's own type.
- **"$4,480,000,000 (approx. closing consideration)".** The $4.48B figure is the amended *purchase price*; Verizon's own accounting gives a closing consideration of about $4.7B. The film labels $4.48B as the amended price and shows the $4.7B figure as the final accounting.
- **"~$3 billion (talks)" for Google** and **the Facebook figure** are shown only as reported accounts, never on a document card.
- **Founders' photo with a CRT.** No freely licensed photo of Yang and Filo together from 1994 was used; see `CREDITS.md` for the photos that are shown.
