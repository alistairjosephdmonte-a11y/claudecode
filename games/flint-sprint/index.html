FLINT SPRINT

Oog the caveman is late for the clan feast. Run the three-lane trail, sidestep boulders and stone pillars, jump fallen logs, duck under bone gates, and grab meat for bonus points. The trail gets faster the longer you last.

  • ← → or A D: change lane
  • ↑, W or Space: jump
  • ↓ or S: duck (in the air: drop fast)
  • P or Esc: pause
  • Touch: swipe, or use the arrow buttons
  • Sound cues: a low buzz means jump, a high chime means duck, and a thud panned left, centre or right marks a blocked lane.
