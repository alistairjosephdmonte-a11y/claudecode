Ink & Echo

Draw lines with limited ink to guide the stick figure to the folded corner of each page.

How to play

The figure walks on its own once you press Run. It climbs gentle slopes, turns around at steep lines and page edges, and falls through gaps. Drawn lines cost ink; erasing a line gives the ink back. While the figure is running you get 3 erasers to rescue a bad run. Use less ink for more stars.

Mouse or touch
Drag to draw a line. Click or tap a blue line to erase it.
Keyboard (focus the page first)
Arrow keys move the cursor one grid square; hold Shift for 5.
Space or Enter sets a line's start, then its end. Esc cancels.
[ and ] select your lines; Delete erases the selected line.
G run or stop, R reset the figure, D describe the page, N next page.
S slow motion, C high contrast.
Grid
The page is 48 columns by 27 rows. Column 0 is the left edge and row 0 is the top; rows count downward.
