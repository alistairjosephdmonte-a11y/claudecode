About the Exhibition

A world built
for dreamers

House of Dreamers is a sensory adventure beyond imagination — a living laboratory where experimentation reigns and every room unlocks a new parallel universe.

Created by Italian designers Elena and Giulia Sella, the exhibition weaves hypnotic light installations, 3D projections, and augmented reality into 18 rooms that blur the line between waking life and dreams.

Dive into a giant ball pool, dance on a multicolored floor, lose yourself in a room of roses, or step through a looking-glass into an upside-down world.

The Experience

Beyond the
limits of reality

Augmented reality, 3D projections, and cutting-edge technology dissolve the boundary between the physical and the imagined. Every surface becomes a canvas, every step a new discovery.

The exhibition is designed to be explored freely — your route is your own. No two journeys are the same.

  • WebGL fluid interactions respond to your movement
  • 3D projection mapping across all surfaces
  • Augmented reality layers unlocked by mobile
  • Generative soundscapes shift in real time
  • Photography-friendly — every corner is a shot
