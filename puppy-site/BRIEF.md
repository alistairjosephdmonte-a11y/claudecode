# Wagtail Hollow Puppy Rescue (imaginary)

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation ("build a site for an imaginary puppy adoption site"). Not interviewed. The organisation, puppies and policies are invented; no real statistics are used.

1. **Vibe:** dusk, warm lamp light, sleepy, tender. Refs: a bedtime picture book, the last scene of a Studio Ghibli film, a porch light.
2. **Journey:** Recognition (someone small is waiting) > Turn (they need you to show up, not to be perfect) > Range (meet five puppies) > Substance (how adoption works) > Peak (the first night, the puppy falls asleep as you scroll) > Commitment (start an adoption chat).
3. **Energy:** calm hero, quiet statement, lively cards, plain process, hushed peak, warm close.
4. **Feeling curve:** hero = wonder (eyes follow you) · statement = tenderness · cards = delight · process = reassurance · peak = release (the room goes quiet, the puppy sleeps) · close = belonging.
   **Peak:** "I scrolled and the puppy fell asleep." Lives in the "first night" act, the largest span.
5. **Signature move:** the puppy's eyes follow your pointer in the hero; in the peak, scroll position drives its eyelids closed while night turns to dawn.
6. **Range:** playful but quiet, illustrated, flat shapes.
7. **Structure:** distinct scenes, not worldflight.
8. **Assets:** none supplied; no image generation key. The world is hand-built SVG illustration, which is a deliberate choice for a storybook brand rather than a photographic one.

Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where the puppy falls asleep while you scroll."
CTA label everywhere: **Start an adoption chat**
