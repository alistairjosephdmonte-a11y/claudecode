# Drop-Day Checklist 👟

Your game plan for copping a limited release, without overpaying.

## A week before
- [ ] **Confirm the details.** Release date, time *and time zone*, retail price, and the exact colorway and style code.
- [ ] **List every place it's dropping.** The brand's own app (e.g. Nike SNKRS, adidas CONFIRMED), big retailers and local boutiques.
- [ ] **Set reminders.** Turn on in-app notifications and add a phone alarm 15 minutes before the drop.
- [ ] **Know your size.** Check the brand's size chart. Sizing can differ between models.

## The days before
- [ ] **Enter every raffle you qualify for.** Many boutiques run online or in-store raffles. Read each one's rules (pickup vs. shipping, deadlines).
- [ ] **Save your payment info and shipping address** in every app you'll use, so checkout is one tap.
- [ ] **Update your apps** and log in ahead of time. Getting logged out on drop day is the classic L.
- [ ] **One account per person.** Using duplicate accounts or bots can get your orders cancelled and your account banned.

## Drop day
- [ ] **Strong connection.** Fast Wi-Fi, a charged phone, and mobile data as a backup.
- [ ] **Be in the app before the drop time.** Don't refresh like crazy. Follow each app's queue or draw instructions.
- [ ] **Check your email and notifications.** Raffle wins often come with a short window to pay.

## If you miss it
- [ ] **Check retail first.** Restocks and shock drops happen, so keep notifications on.
- [ ] **Compare resale prices** across platforms, and include fees and shipping in the total.
- [ ] **Buy authenticated.** Use platforms that verify pairs, and avoid "too good to be true" deals in DMs.
- [ ] **Set a max price and stick to it.** Hype cools down. Prices often dip after the first few weeks, but not always.

---
*Save this and run through it before every drop.*
